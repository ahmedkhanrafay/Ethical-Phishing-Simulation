SECURITY AWARENESS TRAINING

How to Identify Phishing

  • Check the sender's email address carefully.
  • Be cautious of unexpected links or urgent requests.
  • Check links before clicking them.
  • Never share passwords or sensitive information through suspicious messages.
  • When unsure, verify the request through an official channel.
Remember

Stop, check, and verify before clicking.

Training Purpose

This educational content helps users recognize common phishing indicators and develop safer online habits.
